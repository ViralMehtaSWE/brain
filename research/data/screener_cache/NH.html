About

Narayana Hrudayalaya Ltd is engaged in providing economical healthcare services. It has a network of multispecialty and super specialty hospitals spread across multiple locations.[1]

Key Points

Hospital Portfolio[1] [2]
The company is a healthcare service provider in India, operating a network of 55 healthcare facilities across India, along with a presence in the Cayman Islands and the UK. The network includes 20 hospitals and heart centres in India, 20 clinics and dialysis centres, 2 facilities in the Cayman Islands, and 13 facilities in the UK, with 5,955 operational beds and a total capacity of 6,269 beds. The company owns and operates certain hospitals, while others are operated under management agreements, under which Narayana Health acquires operating control of the facilities.
