About

Bharat Petroleum Corporation is a public sector company which is engaged in the business of refining of crude oil and marketing of petroleum products.[1]

Key Points

Refining and Marketing Scale:[1]

BPCL operates the Mumbai, Kochi and Bina refineries with combined capacity of 35.3 MMTPA, around 14% of India's refining capacity. FY26 refinery throughput reached 41.15 MMT, implying sustained operation above nameplate capacity, while distillate yield improved to 84.54%.[1][2]
Domestic petroleum-product sales rose to 54.18 MMT in FY26, giving BPCL a 27.27% market share; the network expanded to 25,323 retail outlets.[1]
In LPG, BPCL had a 27.53% market share, 57 bottling plants, over 6,278 distributors
and approximately 8.6 crore customer connections in FY26.[1]
The wider network also includes industrial customers, aviation stations, lubricant-blending plants and city-gas geographical areas, keeping BPCL present across refining, logistics and multiple fuel-distribution channels.[3]
