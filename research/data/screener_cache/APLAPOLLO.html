About

APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APL Apollo) is one of India’s leading branded steel products manufacturers. Headquartered at Delhi NCR, the Company runs 10 manufacturing facilities churning out over 1,500 varieties of MS Black Pipes, Galvanised Tubes, Pre-Galvanised Tubes, Structural ERW Steel Tubes and Hollow Sections to serve industry applications like urban infrastructures, housing, irrigation, solar plants, greenhouses and engineering. [1]

Key Points

Product Segments:[1]
a) Apollo Structural:
The company offers a range of structural steel tubes for super-heavy, light, and general construction applications. The portfolio includes India's first DFT technology-based structural tubes, lightweight furniture tubes, door frames, planks, and square and rectangular hollow sections. These products are widely used in high-rise buildings, warehouses, infrastructure projects, columns, beams, door frames, staircases, furniture, etc.
