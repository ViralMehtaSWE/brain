About

Godrej Consumer Products is engaged in a fast moving consumer goods Company, manufacturing and marketing Household and Personal Care products.[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1][2]
Godrej Consumer Products Limited (GCPL)
is global fast-moving consumer goods
company serving 1.4 bn consumers. The business is organised around Home Care
and Personal Care categories, with operations across India, Indonesia, Africa, the USA, the Middle East, Latin America and other markets. The portfolio covers household insecticides,
air fresheners, fabric care, toilet cleaners,
soaps, handwash, bodywash, facewash,
hair colour, hair care, perfumes, deodorants,
baby care, wet tissues, and pet food.

GCPL's India business spans Home Care and Personal Care. Its international operations include hair fashion and hair care in Africa, Aer Pocket across Africa, the USA and the Middle East, and household insecticides, shampoo hair colour, baby care and Stella air fresheners in Indonesia. The company also operates a pet care subsidiary, Godrej Pet Care Limited, which manufactures pet food products. [3] [4] [5]
