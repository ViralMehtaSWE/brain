About

Incorporated in 1958, Hindalco Industries Ltd. is a flagship company of the Aditya Birla Group. The Co and its subsidiaries are primarily engaged in the production of Aluminium and Copper. It is also
engaged in the manufacturing of aluminium sheet, extrusion and light gauge products for use in packaging markets like beverage and food, can and foil products, etc. [1]

Key Points

Business Segments:[1]
a) Novelis (63% in Q1 FY27 vs 61% in Q1 FY26):[2] [3][4]
The segment comprises Novelis Inc., a wholly owned foreign subsidiary of the company, along with its step-down subsidiaries, which manufacture and sell aluminium sheet and light-gauge products across North America, South America, Europe, and Asia. Novelis is the world’s leading producer of automotive and beverage can sheet. It is also the world’s largest aluminium recycler. The segment reported a total shipment of 916 KT in Q1 FY27 vs 963 KT in Q1 FY26.
b) Copper (20% in Q1 FY27 vs 23% in Q1 FY26):[2] [5]
The copper business manufactures copper cathodes, copper rods, precious metals and di-ammonium phosphate (DAP). It serves more than half of the country’s refined copper demand and is also the world’s 2nd-largest producer of copper rods.
Sales (KT):[6]
-Metal: 105 in Q1 FY27 vs 124 in Q1 FY26
-CC Rods: 96 in Q1 FY27 vs 104 in Q1 FY26
c) Aluminium (17% in Q1 FY27 vs 16% in Q1 FY26):[7][8] [9]
The aluminium business operates a fully integrated value chain covering bauxite mining,
alumina refining, aluminium smelting, casting and downstream manufacturing. Its product portfolio includes alumina, aluminium ingots, billets, wire rods, and high-value downstream products such as flat rolled products, extrusions, and foils. The company is India’s only fully integrated aluminium player, Asia’s largest producer of primary aluminium and one of the country’s largest downstream aluminium producers.
Sales (KT): [10]
-Upstream: 335 in Q1 FY27 vs 325 in Q1 FY26
-Downstream: 104 in Q1 FY27 vs 101 in Q1 FY26
Downstream Product Mix:[11]
-FRP and Foils: 80% in Q1 FY27 vs 79% in Q1 FY26
-Extrusion: 20% in Q1 FY27 vs 21% in Q1 FY26
d) Specialty Alumina: [9][12]
The company is one of India’s leading manufacturers of specialty alumina and hydrates. Its specialty alumina portfolio, marketed under brands such as PrizTec, InnoSafe and FUSALOX, caters to diverse applications including refractories, ceramics, abrasives, flame retardants, water treatment, etc.
