About

Incorporated in 1986, Indian Railway Finance Corporation borrows funds from the financial markets to finance the acquisition / creation of assets which are then leased out to the Indian Railways as finance lease[1]

Key Points

Business Overview:[1]
The company serves as the funding arm of Indian Railways,
mobilizing funds from domestic and overseas capital markets.
It is a Navratana and a Schedule 'A' Public Sector Enterprise under the administrative control of the Ministry of Railways, Government of India. It is also registered as a Systemically
Important Non-Deposit Taking NBFC and Infrastructure
Finance Company.
