About

IIFL Wealth Management Limited, founded in 2008, is one of the largest private wealth management firms in India. The company mainly acts as wealth manager and provides services relating to financial products distribution, advisory, portfolio management services by mobilizing funds and assets of various classes of investors including High Networth Individuals. [1][2]

Key Points

Business Verticals:
a) Wealth Management: [1][2][3]
The company is one of India’s largest wealth managers. The platform offers discretionary and non-discretionary portfolio management, product distribution across mutual funds, AIFs, PMS and global investments, along with lending, corporate treasury and estate planning services. It provides advice-oriented wealth management solutions for UHNIs, with a primary focus on clients with Rs. 50+ Cr of investable assets. The platform also serves 600+ corporate treasuries and is expanding its footprint to serve the growing HNI segment with Rs. 10-50 Cr in assets.
