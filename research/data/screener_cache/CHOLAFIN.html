About

Cholamandalam Investment & Finance Company is one of the premier diversified non-banking finance companies in India, engaged in providing vehicle finance, home loans and Loan against property.[1]

Key Points

Parentage:[1]
The company is part of the Murugappa Group, a diversified business conglomerate with over 125 years of history. The Group operates across key sectors including Agriculture, Engineering, and Financial Services through a portfolio of 29+ businesses, of which 10 are listed companies. As of FY26, the Group had a market capitalization of approximately US$40.83 Bn and reported consolidated turnover of around US$10.2 Bn in FY25. It has a presence in 50 countries, operates 113 manufacturing locations, and employs more than 94,000 people worldwide.
