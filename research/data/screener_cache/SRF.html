About

Incorporated in 1970, SRF Ltd manufactures and sells technical textiles, chemicals, packaging films, aluminum foils, and other polymers[1]

The chemical segment comprises 2 product divisions, including:
a) Speciality Chemicals:
The company offers a portfolio of organic

(–CClF₂), difluoro (–CHF₂), and hexafluoro
((–CF₃)₂) products, catering to applications across material sciences, surface chemistry, agrochemicals, and pharmaceuticals.

It manufactures refrigerants (such as R22
and HFC blends), pharmaceutical propellants, chloromethanes, hydrogen fluoride (HF), and other fluorine-based chemicals.
