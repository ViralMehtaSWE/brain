About

Founded in 2014, Swiggy Ltd is a new-age, consumer-first technology company offering users an easy-to-use convenience platform, accessible through a unified app.[1]

Key Points

Business Model:[1]
Swiggy runs a hyperlocal commerce platform covering food delivery, quick commerce, dining out and events, along with B2B supply chain and distribution services.
Its consumer businesses largely follow a marketplace model, connecting customers
with restaurants and merchants. Company's revenue comes from partner commissions, advertising, user and platform fees, and subscriptions. The B2B business also
earns from the sale of goods and
supply chain services.
